01 / UNDERSTANDING OVER SHORTCUTS

Catch the mistake, don’t just copy the answer.

Spitting out a final number doesn't save you on test day. AXIOM shows the underlying theorem or rule at every step—and lets you snap a photo of your handwritten work to pinpoint the exact line where a sign error happened.

Built for real conceptual mastery

No blurred steps when finals week hits.

Legacy math apps hook you in and lock step-by-step solutions behind $15/month subscriptions right before midterms. By running symbolic algebra, calculus, and stats engines directly in your browser, AXIOM stays fast, private, and free.
